# Publishing your agent for others

You can publish your agent for others on the **\[ Agents] > \[ Community ]** tab. To publish here, you must modify your agent's **Discoverability** setting on its profile. There are three settings:

* Default is **Private**, only you or the organization it was created in
* **Unlisted** won't show up in the Community tab, but you can direct link others
* **Public** shows up in the community tab
* See: [Modifying your agent > Profile](modifying-your-agent/profile.md) for more information
